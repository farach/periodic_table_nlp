## Decisions Made

- The periodic table is a mnemonic teaching map, not a scientific taxonomy.
- Linked means a checked lesson exists; it does not mean human approval or
  scientific consensus.
- Public renders use dated or local fixtures instead of live API or model
  calls.
- Reference labels are versioned judgments, never ground truth.
- Package teaching fixtures are described as schema examples unless upstream
  identifiers authenticate them.
- Synthetic rows retain source family, template, package commit, codebook,
  review status, and intended split.
- AI-writing scans look for residue and repeated patterns; they do not claim to
  identify authorship.
- Human editorial and real screen-reader reviews remain distinct from automated
  checks.
